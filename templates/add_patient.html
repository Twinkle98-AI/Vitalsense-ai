Privacy & AI Architecture

VitalSense AI enforces strict medical data segregation. When generating health remarks:

  • Zero Identifiers: Name, DOB, and email are never transmitted to NVIDIA NIM.
  • Numeric Only: Only the quantitative lab measurements and units are sent.
  • Atomic Persistence: Records are saved in SQLite only if AI generation succeeds.
